Perfect Engine Launcher 0.2.4-r1

Copy the 3ds folder to the root of your SD card. Place your own legally
backed-up US Rev 1 Perfect Dark ROM at:
/3ds/perfect-engine-launcher/pd.z64

Keep the .3dsx and .smdh files together. Launch through Homebrew Launcher.
Requires New 3DS / New 2DS and DSP firmware at /3ds/dspfirm.cdc.

No ROM or extracted game files are included. Settings are saved in pd.ini.
Wi-Fi enables optional startup update checks. You choose whether to install.
Create no-update-check.flag beside the launcher to turn those checks off.

This is an early port. The full campaign has not been tested. Expect bugs,
rendering issues, freezes and performance drops. See CREDITS.txt and licenses/.
Source: https://github.com/Raihwy3/perfect-engine-launcher
