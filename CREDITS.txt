Perfect Engine Launcher — credits

Engine
Ryan Dwyer and the Perfect Dark decompilation contributors, and the Perfect Dark
PC port contributors. The pinned engine source is MIT licensed; the Fast3D
renderer retains its separate notices.
Decompilation: https://github.com/n64decomp/perfect_dark
PC port: https://github.com/perfect-dark-pc-port/perfect_dark

Eyes of Gold
Raihwy3 and Eyes of Gold contributors: the PICA/Citro3D renderer, platform
adapters, DSP/audio queue work, stereo depth controls, touchscreen/menu tools,
gyro and flick-stick integration, crosshair controls and launcher updater.
The adapted sources keep their MIT notices. The Eyes of Gold code also builds
on the GoldenEye PC port and Fast3D work credited in the renderer notices.
https://github.com/Raihwy3/eyes-of-gold
https://github.com/jkdansereau/goldeneye-pc-port

Motion controls
Julian "Jibb" Smart and contributors: GamepadMotionHelpers for motion fusion,
orientation spaces and calibration; JoyShockMapper for adapted flick-stick
timing and rotation smoothing. Both MIT licenses are included.
https://github.com/JibbSmart/GamepadMotionHelpers
https://github.com/JibbSmart/JoyShockMapper

Stereoscopic rendering
OneUp (oneup03) and RT64 contributors: dynamic3d guidance and stereo depth /
convergence calculations adapted from rt64-3ds. The RT64 MIT license is included.
This port uses a separate PICA renderer.
https://github.com/oneup03/3D-Skills/tree/main/dynamic3d
https://github.com/oneup03/rt64-3ds

Updater
Adapted from Eyes of Gold. Uses curl, Mbed TLS, zlib/minizip and Serge Zaitsev's
jsmn parser. The embedded trust store is Mozilla's root certificate collection,
distributed through curl's CA extract. Original notices and licenses are included.
https://curl.se/
https://github.com/Mbed-TLS/mbedtls
https://zlib.net/
https://github.com/zserge/jsmn
https://curl.se/docs/caextract.html

Tools and runtime
The devkitPro contributors: devkitARM, libctru, Citro3D, picasso, smdhtool and
3dsxtool. GCC and newlib provide compiler/runtime support. Their applicable
runtime licenses and the GCC Runtime Library Exception are included.
Mark Adler: the public-domain inflate core; its provenance comments remain in
vendor/public-domain-inflate. The original launcher icon is drawn by
 tools/make_launcher_icon.py (optional Pillow dependency).
https://devkitpro.org/
https://github.com/devkitPro/libctru
https://github.com/devkitPro/citro3d

rt64-3ds renderer in Raihwy3's Conker: 3DS Recomp, with credit to RT64
contributors and the Eyes of Gold implementation those shapes evolved from.
See vendor/conker-reticle/source.json and licenses/conker-reticle.txt.
